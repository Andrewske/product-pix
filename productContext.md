# Product Context

## Problem Space
E-commerce businesses need high-quality product photos but professional photography is expensive and time-consuming.

## Solution
An AI tool that takes product images and prompts to generate professional settings/backgrounds.

## User Experience
- **Visual Style**: High contrast, dark backgrounds, "Dark + Warm" palette (Deep Black, Electric Coral, Cream, Mint).
- **Key Principles**: Generous whitespace, sharp corners, product photos as heroes.
