# Account Management

Manage your ProductPix account, update your profile, and control your subscription settings.

## Profile Settings

Access your profile at **Settings > Profile**:

<Steps>
<Step number={1} title="Update Display Name">
Change the name displayed on your account. This appears in shared galleries and team features.
</Step>

<Step number={2} title="Update Email">
Change your account email. You'll receive a verification link to confirm the new address.
</Step>

<Step number={3} title="Update Avatar">
Upload a profile picture to personalize your account.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Changing Your Password

<Callout variant="warning" title="Security">
Choose a strong, unique password. We recommend using a password manager.
</Callout>

To change your password:

1. Go to **Settings > Security**
2. Enter your current password
3. Enter and confirm your new password
4. Click **Update Password**

### Password Requirements
- Minimum 8 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character

## Forgot Password

If you've forgotten your password:

1. Go to the [Login page](/login)
2. Click **Forgot Password**
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your inbox for a reset link
5. Create a new password

<Callout variant="info">
Reset links expire after 1 hour for security. Request a new one if needed.
</Callout>

## Billing & Subscriptions

### Viewing Billing History

Access your complete billing history at **Settings > Billing**:

- All past invoices and receipts
- Credit purchase history
- Subscription payment history
- Download invoices as PDF

### Managing Your Subscription

From **Settings > Billing**, you can:

- **View current plan** and renewal date
- **Upgrade** to a higher tier
- **Downgrade** to a lower tier (takes effect next billing cycle)
- **Cancel** your subscription
- **Update payment method**

### Updating Payment Method

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**
2. Click **Payment Methods**
3. Add a new card or update existing
4. Set as default payment method

<Callout variant="tip">
Keep your payment method up to date to avoid service interruption.
</Callout>

## Download Your Data

You can export your data at any time:

1. Go to **Settings > Privacy**
2. Click **Export My Data**
3. We'll prepare your export and email you when ready
4. Download includes: profile info, generation history, settings

Export typically takes 24-48 hours for large accounts.

## Account Deletion

<Callout variant="warning" title="Permanent Action">
Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All data will be permanently removed.
</Callout>

To delete your account:

1. Go to **Settings > Privacy**
2. Click **Delete Account**
3. Enter your password to confirm
4. Confirm deletion in the modal

### What Gets Deleted
- Your profile and settings
- All generated images
- Generation history
- Payment history (after legal retention period)

### What's Not Deleted
- Invoices (retained for tax/legal compliance)
- Anonymized usage analytics

### Before Deleting
- Download any images you want to keep
- Export your data if needed
- Cancel any active subscriptions first

## Two-Factor Authentication

Add an extra layer of security with 2FA:

<Steps>
<Step number={1} title="Enable 2FA">
Go to **Settings > Security** and click **Enable 2FA**.
</Step>

<Step number={2} title="Scan QR Code">
Use an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password) to scan the QR code.
</Step>

<Step number={3} title="Enter Verification Code">
Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app to confirm setup.
</Step>

<Step number={4} title="Save Backup Codes">
Download and securely store your backup codes. Use these if you lose access to your authenticator.
</Step>
</Steps>
