# Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ProductPix.

## Generation Questions

### Why did my generation fail?

<Callout variant="info">
Failed generations are automatically refunded. Check your credit balance to confirm.
</Callout>

Common reasons for failures:

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Image too small | Source under 512px | Use a higher resolution source |
| Invalid format | Unsupported file type | Convert to PNG, JPG, or WebP |
| Timeout | Complex prompt or high load | Try again, simplify prompt |
| Content policy | Prohibited content detected | Review our content guidelines |

### How long does generation take?

- **Standard resolution**: 10-20 seconds
- **High resolution**: 15-30 seconds
- **Ultra resolution**: 20-45 seconds

Times may vary based on system load and prompt complexity.

### Can I edit a generated image?

Currently, you cannot edit generated images directly. To make changes:
1. Download the generated image
2. Edit in your preferred image editor
3. Re-upload if you need further AI processing

### What image formats are supported?

**Upload**: PNG, JPG/JPEG, WebP (max 10MB)
**Download**: PNG, JPG, WebP

### What's the maximum image size?

- **Upload**: 10MB file size, 4096px max dimension
- **Output**: Up to 2048px (Ultra resolution)

## Credit & Billing Questions

### Do credits expire?

<Callout variant="success">
No! Credits never expire. Use them whenever you need them.
</Callout>

### Can I get a refund?

**Automatic refunds**: Failed generations due to system errors are automatically refunded.

**Manual refunds**: Contact support for duplicate charges or technical issues. We cannot refund successful generations.

### What happens when I run out of credits?

You can:
1. Purchase a credit pack
2. Upgrade your subscription
3. Wait for your monthly subscription credits to renew

### Can I upgrade mid-subscription?

Yes! When you upgrade:
- Immediate access to new features
- Prorated credits for the billing period
- New price starts immediately

### How do I cancel my subscription?

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**
2. Click **Manage Subscription**
3. Select **Cancel Subscription**
4. Confirm cancellation

You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

## Account Questions

### How do I reset my password?

1. Go to the [Login page](/login)
2. Click **Forgot Password**
3. Enter your email
4. Check your inbox and follow the link

### Can I change my email address?

Yes, in **Settings > Profile**. You'll need to verify the new email address.

### Is my data secure?

<Callout variant="info" title="Security">
We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data.
</Callout>

- All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Images stored encrypted at rest
- SOC 2 Type II compliant infrastructure
- Regular security audits

### Who can see my generated images?

Only you can see your images by default. Images are:
- Private to your account
- Not used for training
- Not shared with third parties
- Deletable at any time

## Troubleshooting

### Images won't upload

- Check file size (max 10MB)
- Verify format (PNG, JPG, WebP)
- Try a different browser
- Disable browser extensions

### Generations are slow

- Check your internet connection
- Try during off-peak hours
- Use standard resolution for faster results

### Can't access my account

1. Try resetting your password
2. Clear browser cache and cookies
3. Try a different browser
4. Contact support if issues persist
