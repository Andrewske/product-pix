# Single Image Generation

Create stunning product photos with our AI-powered generation tool. This guide covers everything you need to know about generating individual images.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Best Results">
Start with a high-quality product photo on a plain background for the best AI transformations.
</Callout>

## Step-by-Step Workflow

<Steps>
<Step number={1} title="Upload Your Product Image">
Navigate to the [Generate page](/generate) and upload your product photo. We support PNG, JPG, and WebP formats up to 10MB.

For best results:
- Use a well-lit photo with even lighting
- Place your product on a plain white or neutral background
- Capture the product from your desired angle
- Ensure the image is in focus and high resolution
</Step>

<Step number={2} title="Describe Your Scene">
Enter a description of the scene you want. Be specific about:
- **Background**: "marble countertop", "outdoor garden setting", "minimalist studio"
- **Lighting**: "soft natural light", "dramatic shadows", "bright and airy"
- **Style**: "luxury", "lifestyle", "e-commerce white background"
</Step>

<Step number={3} title="Choose Your Settings">
Select your output preferences:
- **Aspect Ratio**: Choose from 1:1 (square), 4:3, 16:9, or custom ratios
- **Resolution**: Higher resolutions use more credits but produce sharper images
</Step>

<Step number={4} title="Generate">
Click **Generate** and wait for your image. Most generations complete in 15-30 seconds depending on complexity.
</Step>
</Steps>

Choose the aspect ratio that best fits your use case:

| Ratio | Best For |
|-------|----------|
| 1:1 | Instagram, product catalogs |
| 4:3 | E-commerce listings |
| 16:9 | Website banners, hero images |
| 9:16 | Stories, mobile displays |

## Resolution Tiers & Credit Costs

Higher resolutions produce sharper images but cost more credits:

| Resolution | Credits | Best For |
|------------|---------|----------|
| Standard (1024px) | 1 credit | Social media, quick previews |
| High (1536px) | 2 credits | E-commerce, web use |
| Ultra (2048px) | 4 credits | Print, large displays |

## Tips for Best Results

<Callout variant="info" title="Prompt Tips">
Be descriptive but concise. Include details about lighting, background, and style. Avoid vague terms like "nice" or "good".
</Callout>

### Do's
- Use clear, specific descriptions
- Mention the type of surface or background you want
- Specify lighting conditions
- Include style keywords (luxury, minimal, rustic, etc.)

### Don'ts
- Don't use overly long prompts (keep under 200 characters)
- Don't include text or logos in your descriptions
- Don't expect exact reproductions of copyrighted styles

## Saving & Downloading

After generation:
1. **Preview** your image in the result panel
2. **Download** in your preferred format
3. **Save to gallery** to access later from your dashboard
4. **Favorite** images to find them quickly
