# Credits & Pricing

ProductPix uses a simple credit system for image generation. This guide explains how credits work and helps you choose the right plan.

<Callout variant="success" title="Credits Never Expire">
Once purchased, your credits never expire. Use them whenever you're ready!
</Callout>

## How Credits Work

Every image generation costs credits based on the output resolution:

| Resolution | Size | Credits | Best For |
|------------|------|---------|----------|
| Standard | 1024px | 1 credit | Social media, previews |
| High | 1536px | 2 credits | E-commerce, websites |
| Ultra | 2048px | 4 credits | Print, large displays |

### Example Costs

- **10 social media images** (standard): 10 credits
- **20 e-commerce photos** (high): 40 credits
- **5 hero banners** (ultra): 20 credits

## Viewing Your Balance

Check your credit balance anytime:

1. **Dashboard**: Credit count shown in the header
2. **Settings > Billing**: Detailed balance and history
3. **Before generation**: Cost shown before you generate

## Transaction History

View all credit activity in **Settings > Billing**:

- Credit purchases
- Subscription renewals
- Generation costs
- Refunds (if applicable)

## Refund Policy

<Callout variant="info" title="Generation Refunds">
If a generation fails due to a system error, credits are automatically refunded within minutes.
</Callout>

### What's Refundable
- Failed generations (system errors)
- Duplicate charges
- Technical issues preventing use

### What's Not Refundable
- Successful generations (even if you don't like the result)
- User errors (wrong prompt, wrong image)
- Unused subscription credits after cancellation

## Choosing the Right Plan

### You Should Buy Credit Packs If:
- Occasional or seasonal use
- Testing ProductPix for the first time
- Unpredictable generation needs
- One-time projects

### You Should Subscribe If:
- Regular monthly generation needs
- Predictable volume (e-commerce stores)
- Need API access
- Want priority support

## FAQ

**Can I upgrade my subscription mid-cycle?**
Yes! You'll receive prorated credits for the remainder of your billing period.

**What happens if I run out of credits?**
You can purchase additional credit packs anytime, even with an active subscription.

**Do credits transfer between accounts?**
No, credits are tied to your account and cannot be transferred.
