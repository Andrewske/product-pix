# Getting Started with ProductPix

Welcome to ProductPix! This guide will help you create stunning product photos using AI in just a few minutes.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Quick Start">
Upload a product image, choose a style, and generate - it's that simple!
</Callout>

## What is ProductPix?

ProductPix is an AI-powered tool that transforms your product photos into professional-quality images. Simply upload your product image, choose a style, and let our AI generate beautiful, studio-quality photos.

## Quick Start Guide

<Steps>
<Step number={1} title="Upload your product image">
Use a clear photo of your product against a simple background. PNG or JPG formats work best, and higher resolution images produce better results.
</Step>

<Step number={2} title="Choose your style">
Select from our library of professional backgrounds and lighting setups. You can also describe a custom scene in the prompt field.
</Step>

<Step number={3} title="Generate">
Click the Generate button and watch AI transform your image. Most generations complete in under 30 seconds.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding Credits

<Callout variant="info">
Credits never expire! Purchase what you need and use them whenever you're ready.
</Callout>

ProductPix uses a credit system for image generation:

- Each generation costs credits based on the resolution
- Higher resolutions use more credits but produce sharper images
- You can purchase credit packs or subscribe for monthly credits
